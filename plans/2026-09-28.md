## Saturday — Functional circuits

### Round 1 — Base + power
Rest 60–75 s, repeat the round 3 times.

1. Goblet squat + press — 12 kg kettlebell · 12 reps
   Hold the kettlebell with both hands at your chest. Squat until thighs are parallel to the floor, knees out, back straight. As you stand up, press the kettlebell overhead.
2. Burpee (simplified) — 12 reps
   From standing, squat and put your hands on the floor, jump your feet back to a plank, do 1 push-up, jump your feet back to your hands, jump up with a clap overhead.
3. Plank shoulder taps — 20 taps (10 each hand)
   High plank on straight arms, feet a bit wider than shoulders. Tap your left shoulder with your right hand, then the right with your left. Keep your hips still, don't rock.

### Round 2 — Legs + core in motion
Rest 60–75 s, repeat the round 3 times.

1. Lunge with torso twist — 5–8 kg plate or dumbbell · 10 each leg
   Hold the weight in front of you. Step forward into a lunge (front knee at 90°, back knee almost touching the floor), and at the bottom twist your torso toward the front leg. Return to start.
2. Mountain climbers — 35 seconds, fast
   High plank, hands under shoulders, body in a straight line. Quickly drive your knees to your chest one after another, like running in place. Don't lift your hips.
3. Ball slams — 15 reps
   Hold a ball (or plate) overhead on straight arms. Using your whole body (abs and shoulders), slam it into the floor in front of you as hard as you can. Pick it up and repeat.

### Round 3 — Finisher burn
Rest 60–75 s, repeat the round 3 times.

1. Squat jump — 15 reps
   Feet shoulder-width apart. Squat until thighs are parallel to the floor, then jump up as high as you can, using your arms. Land softly, bending your knees, and go straight into the next squat.
2. Renegade row — 6–8 kg dumbbells · 10 each arm
   High plank holding two dumbbells, feet a bit wider for balance. Row one dumbbell to your hip, elbow back, then the other. Don't twist your hips.
3. Russian twist with ball — 20 reps (10 each side)
   Sit with feet slightly off the floor (or on the floor if that's too hard), lean back about 45°. Hold the ball with both hands and twist right and left, touching the floor beside you.
